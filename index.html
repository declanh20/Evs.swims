Who I Am
The Story
So Far

Hi, I'm Evany — a 12-year-old swimmer from Queensland who eats, sleeps, and breathes the pool. Breaststroke is where I belong, but freestyle is where I'm building, and every race is a chance to find out what I'm made of.

I train with Trinity Aquatic and have already made my mark at the Queensland level — picking up a Silver medal in the 200m Breaststroke and placing in the Top 10 across my breaststroke events. None of it came easy, and that's exactly why it means so much.

Early mornings, tired arms, and a lot of chlorine — that's the reality behind the results. But I wouldn't trade it. Every lap I swim is closing the gap between where I am and where I want to be: the Australian Age Championships.

Follow along for the real journey — not just the wins, but the work, the nerves, the breakthrough moments, and the grind it takes to compete at the highest level as a junior Australian swimmer. 💜
